I • Echoes of the Arbor-Basin & The Unseen Enemy

Blood Beneath the Petrified Canopy

High in low-planetary orbit hangs The Sanctuary of Ash—a scarred, acid-scrubbed battle barge crewed by a condemned regiment of the Death Korps of Krieg. You remember them well. Before their heavy siege tanks were permitted to touch the soil of the Golgotha Arbor-Basin (Sector Node 0), it was your Adepta Sororitas Wardens who met them beneath the petrified jungle canopy and subjected them to a trial of fire and blood.

Your Exorcists and war-engines burned that day under the merciless salvos of their Vanquisher cannons—the tanks their trench-rats now whisper of in the dark as "The Shrine-Breakers." Yet when the smoke cleared and every Krieg officer lay broken in the mud, it was Saint Celestine, the Ashen Seraph, who descended from the storm-clouds into their rear lines, slaughtering their command echelon and claiming the field by a razor's edge while Aestred Thurga & Agathae Dolan inscribed their sins into the sacred tapestry.

👁️‍🗨️ The Voice in the Ash (Your Chronicler & The Third Force)
Now the prologue has ended, and the true war for Armageddon begins. Whether you return to the surface at the head of the Sisterhood of the High Sanctum to finish your vigil, or descend from the void commanding an entirely new warhost drawn by the scent of relics and slaughter—know that you are not alone.

I am the Chronicler of this war—recording every scar, every fallen hero, and every legend your warriors forge—and I am also your enemy. In the uncharted fog beyond the forest canopy, a Third Force is stirring. We have no known flagship in orbit. Whether we claw our way up from subterranean brood-tunnels, erupt from screaming tears in the Warp, or march out of the choking ash-storms, my legions will contest every inch of this world against both you and the Krieg. When you two lock blades in the ashes, I will feast upon the aftermath. And when my hordes surround you both in the dark, you will have to decide whether the commander standing beside you is your salvation... or your next target.
