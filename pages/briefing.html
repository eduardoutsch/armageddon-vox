Campaign Synergy Rules • Mode B → Administratum Roster

Spoils of Breach Containment

Completing your confidential Secret Objective, extracting Fortifications, and banking leftover Supply Points in Horde Mode permanently strengthens your Crusade Order of Battle.

1. The Traitor's Bounty (Covert Betrayal Payout)

Completing a Secret Objective carrying the Traitor tag in Co-op Horde Mode requires orchestrating the downfall of your partner amidst the breach. Because of the extreme risk and the permanent grudge it creates, fulfilling a Traitor Directive awards a Double Spoils Payout:

  • Plundered Command Cache: Steal 1 Requisition Point (RP) (or 2 Strategic Points) directly from the betrayed Commander, AND expand your Crusade Force's Supply Limit by +200 pts for free.
  • Forbidden Spoils of Treachery: Bestow a free Battle Honour (or an Armageddon Antiquity Relic ignoring rank restrictions!) upon your Warlord or the unit that executed the betrayal, plus +3 bonus XP.
  • Shipboard Shift (The Exile // Sanctuary of Ash Only): Completing a Traitor directive shifts The Sanctuary of Ash Balance Slider 1 step toward the Lower Decks (-1).
  • The Grudge Consequence: In your next 1v1 PvP clash, the betrayed Commander automatically receives 1 free Armageddon Crusade Blessing even if they are not the Underdog!

2. Standard Secret Objective Victor Bonuses

Completing any non-Traitor Secret Objective (and surviving Round 6 if required) awards +5 Strategic Points, +1 bonus RP (or +100 pts Supply Limit), plus the reward matching your card's tag:

  • Action / Relic Directives: The unit that completed the Action earns a free Battle Trait or Weapon Modification (or a free Crusade Relic if a Character) without needing to rank up.
  • Kill / Combat Directives: Select one additional unit to be Marked for Greatness (+3 XP) at the end of the battle.
  • Support / Fortify / Extraction Directives: Claim a free Repair and Recuperate Requisition after the battle (heal 1 Battle Scar on any unit for 0 RP).

3. Field Promotions, Leftover SP & Persistent Fortifications

  • "A Name Earned" (1 SP) Discount: If a Character buys A Name Earned during Horde Mode, survives Round 6, and slays a Horde Character/Monster/Vehicle (or completes an Action), reduce the RP cost of making that Enhancement permanent via Renowned Heroes by 1 RP (min 1 RP).
  • Recruiting Reinforcements: Any unit summoned via Reinforcements Arrive (12 SP) or Field Promotion (X SP) that survives Round 6 can be inducted directly into your Order of Battle starting with 3 XP!
  • Leftover SP Salvage: Unspent SP after surviving Round 6 can be cashed in: 2 SP = Orbital Auspex Recon Probe into an adjacent Fog-of-War Sector Node; 3 SP = Bank a 1v1 Supply Cache Token; 5 SP = +2 XP to a surviving unit; 8 SP = +1 RP or +100 pts Supply Limit.
  • Extracted Fortifications: Still controlling a Fortified Objective at the end of Round 6 grants a sector bonus: Field Hospital (all destroyed units auto-pass Out of Action tests), Ammo Dump (free Weapon Mod or +2 XP), War Trench (+1 XP to surviving Infantry/Battleline), or FOB (+1 bonus SPt & +1 Sector Hegemony Pip).
